Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

MyDealsPortal is designed to help consumers discover offers from local businesses and to let businesses manage their own listings and deals.

Information we may collect

We may collect account information, business profile information, deal content, payment-related account identifiers, device/browser information, usage analytics, and information you voluntarily submit through forms.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed through Stripe. Payment-related identifiers and subscription status may be used to administer business accounts. Full card details are handled by the payment processor rather than published on MyDealsPortal.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate the service, authenticate users, publish business listings and deals, process subscriptions, provide support, prevent abuse, improve the site, and measure usage.

Public business information

Business names, descriptions, contact information, websites, locations or service areas, and active deals may be displayed publicly when submitted for publication.

Cookies and analytics

The service may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, and analytics.

Data retention and deletion

We may retain information as reasonably necessary to operate the service, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. Account deletion options and data-request procedures will be provided before public launch.

Google account connection for outreach operations

The operator is preparing a separate, authorized connection to a Google account for outreach administration. Proposed access includes read-only Gmail messages and read-only Google Sheets data. This connection is not a consumer-facing MyDealsPortal feature. The automation must be configured to restrict access, avoid publishing message contents, and handle any retention or deletion requests appropriately. The connection is not yet confirmed as operational.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the MyDealsPortal contact page.

This policy is under review. Data handling descriptions should be confirmed against deployed services before it is finalized.
