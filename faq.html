Frequently asked questions

Local deals, discounts and business advertising: FAQs

Looking for nearby specials or a simple way to promote your business? MyDealsPortal helps people discover business-posted deals and gives participating businesses a place to list promotions for a flat monthly fee.

Finding local deals

Where can I find local deals and discounts in Maine?

Visit MyDealsPortal and search by a Maine ZIP code, keyword or category to see deals that participating businesses have published. Results depend on which businesses currently have active listings, so availability varies by location.

How do I find restaurant specials or other deals near me?

Enter your ZIP code and try a keyword such as restaurant, lunch, automotive or salon, or browse the available categories. MyDealsPortal searches published deals; it does not guarantee an offer in every area or category.

Does MyDealsPortal offer coupons?

Businesses can publish discounts, specials and promotional deals. Some offers may resemble coupons, but each listing sets out its own terms. Check the deal details before visiting or attempting to redeem an offer.

How do consumers search for deals?

Search by keyword, ZIP code, category, or combine keyword and ZIP for a more specific result.

Do customers need an account to browse?

No. Anyone can browse public deals without creating an account.

Advertising deals for businesses

Where can a small business advertise local deals online?

MyDealsPortal lets eligible businesses publish promotions that customers can discover through location and category searches. See the business advertising page for details on getting started.

How much does MyDealsPortal cost a business?

The Launch 100 offer gives the first 100 paid businesses a locked $44.99/month rate while continuously subscribed. Month 1 is paid; months 2 and 3 are free. Business #101 onward is $49.99/month, with month 2 free. Review the current offer and terms before subscribing.

Does MyDealsPortal take a commission on sales?

No. The platform uses a flat monthly advertising fee rather than taking a percentage of each customer transaction.

How many promotions can my business post?

Businesses with an active subscription can publish unlimited legitimate deals under the platform's terms.

Can I update or pause my promotions?

Yes. Businesses manage their own offers from the self-service dashboard.

Do deals need approval before appearing?

Deals can be prepared as drafts before payment. After payment is confirmed and the business is activated, eligible deals become publicly visible. MyDealsPortal may remove fraudulent, abusive, illegal, impersonating or spam listings.

Does advertising guarantee new customers or sales?

No. MyDealsPortal provides a place to publish discoverable deals, but results depend on customer demand, the offer, location and other factors. No particular number of views, redemptions or sales is guaranteed.
